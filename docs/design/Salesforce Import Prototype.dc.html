Before we connect
You'll sign in to Salesforce next.
We look at
•Your tasks and activity from the last 60 days
•The accounts they're attached to
•Your Salesforce name and email
We copy, once
•Those accounts, as companies
•The activity, as notes or action items
A one-time copy, not a sync.
Halosight won't change anything in Salesforce or check back on its own.
What gets imported?{{ detailChevron }}
Halosight reads your recent tasks, groups them by the account each one is attached to, and turns them into account notes or action items. Activity with no account attached is left behind. If fewer than 100 records turn up, we widen the window to 120 days.
You'll approve what we found before anything is copied.
